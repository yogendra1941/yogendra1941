## Fonts
No third-party font files are embedded. SVGs use system font stacks (Inter, system-ui, Segoe UI, and platform monospace fallbacks). The SVGs do not request external fonts or assets, so there are no bundled font binaries or third-party font license files. This intentionally prioritizes self-contained, offline rendering over base64 WOFF2 font embedding.
